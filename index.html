    Our Story

    From Suhareka
    to the world.

    Frutex was founded in 1994 as a family business in Suhareka, Kosovo — a small operation built on big ambition.

    Today, we ship to 35+ markets across six continents, powered by a team of over 500 employees. Every product is guided by one promise: quality, freshness, and a deep respect for natural ingredients.
