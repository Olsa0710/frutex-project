Product Overview

Everything you need to know about Kosovo's most iconic energy drink.

Story & History

Launched in 2018 representing multi-fruit innovation.

Flavor Profile

A bold, well-balanced energy drink with a crisp citrus-forward taste and subtle berry undertones. Slightly carbonated with a smooth finish — never overpowering or medicinal. Available in Classic, Sugar Free, and Tropical variants.

Ingredients

Carbonated water, sugar, citric acid, taurine (0.4%), caffeine (0.03%), natural flavors, B-vitamins (B3, B5, B6, B12), color E150d. Sugar Free variant uses sucralose and steviol glycosides.

Brand Highlights

Official energy drink of Granit Xhaka. Available in Classic, Sugar Free, and Tropical. Exported to 25+ countries. The flagship brand that defined Frutex on the international stage. Featured at major sporting events including off-road racing and basketball.
