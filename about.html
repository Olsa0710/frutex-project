Our Journey

From a family workshop
to a global brand.

Founded in 1994 by the Frutex family in the small town of Suhareka, Kosovo — the company began as a modest workshop producing natural juices from locally grown fruit. What started with a handful of employees and a single production line has grown into something extraordinary.

Today, Frutex operates from a state-of-the-art facility, employs over 500 people, and exports premium beverages to 35+ markets across six continents — all while staying true to the values that built it: quality, integrity, and a deep connection to the community.

Our Mission

To craft premium beverages that bring people together — rooted in Kosovo's finest ingredients, driven by innovation, and delivered with pride to every corner of the globe.

Every bottle that leaves our facility carries the same promise: quality you can taste, freshness you can feel, and a commitment to the communities we serve.

"We don't just make drinks. We create moments — from the first sip of a Golden Eagle to the last drop of a Natural 100 juice."
