Our Journey

From a family workshop
to a global brand.

Founded in 1994 in Suhareka, Kosovo — Frutex began as a modest family workshop producing natural juices from locally grown fruit.

Today we operate from a state-of-the-art facility with 500+ employees, exporting to 35+ markets worldwide.
